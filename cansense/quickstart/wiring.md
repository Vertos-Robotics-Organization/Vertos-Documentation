# Wiring

{% hint style="info" %}
**Warning:** The silkscreen label is incorrect. The board requires **5 V only**. Do not apply 12 V, as this may cause permanent damage.
{% endhint %}

CAN can be wired in daisy chain and non-daisy chain configurations.
