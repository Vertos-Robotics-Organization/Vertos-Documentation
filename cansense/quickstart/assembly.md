# Assembly

## Tools Required

* 1/16" allen wrench
* Phillips #1 screwdriver

## Steps

1. Fasten the housing (6) to the mechanism using two #3 screws (7).
2. Assemble parts (1–5). For best results, first assemble parts (1, 3–5), then align the connector on part (2) before inserting the screw.
